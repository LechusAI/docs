## What this page is for

The **Users** page is where you manage the customers who use your White Label app (the people who buy subscriptions and log in to your product).

From here, you can:

- Create customer accounts
- Search and review users in your workspace
- Open a customer’s app directly (useful for support and verification)
- Check key user details (last login, subscriptions, minutes, UTM attribution)
- Access billing actions that are available for that user (depending on your setup)

To open this page, click here: [Open Users](https://app.callin.io/cms/users)

> **Tip:** Some labels and options can vary depending on your app language and plan.

## How the Users list works

At the top you have a **search bar** to quickly find a customer (by name or email).

In the list, each row represents one customer and typically shows:

- The user’s **name and email**
- A status badge like **Active**
- A **Joined At** date
- A set of **action icons** on the right side of the row

## Create a new customer user

To create a customer account:

1. Click **Create User** (top right).
2. In the “Create New User” modal, fill in:
   - **Email*** (required)
   - **Password*** (required — the modal notes a minimum of 8 characters)
   - **Full Name** (optional)
   - **Company** (optional)
3. Click **Create User** to finish.

> **Common mistake:** Creating a user with a temporary password and forgetting to store it safely. Treat customer credentials carefully and share them through a secure channel.

## Edit a customer’s details

From the user row, click the **pencil icon** to open the edit dialog.

In the “Edit User” modal you can update:

- **Full Name**
- **Company**

Then click **Update User** to save changes.

> **Tip:** Keeping the customer name/company tidy is useful when you’re managing many accounts (for example, when an agency has multiple clients under similar names).

## Open the customer app from the green icon

On the far right of each user row, you’ll see a **green icon** (the last icon in the row).  
This opens the customer’s platform directly, so you can verify what they see or help them troubleshoot.

A typical support flow looks like this:

- Customer says: “I can’t find my subscription page”
- You open their app from the green icon
- You confirm whether the subscription area is visible and whether they’re logged into the correct account

> **Important:** What you see after opening the customer app can depend on that user’s subscription status and your White Label configuration.

## Review a customer’s profile tabs (Info, Subscriptions, 1-Min Billing, UTM)

When you open a customer’s profile view, you’ll typically see tabs like:

- **Info**
- **Subscriptions**
- **1-Min Billing**
- **UTM**

Below is what each tab is used for, based on what’s visible in the UI.

### Info

The **Info** tab includes “Last Login Details,” which helps you understand recent access:

- **Device** (example shown: Desktop – macOS – Firefox)
- **Location** (example shown: Madrid, Madrid, ES)
- **IP Address**
- **Time** (example shown: 3 days ago)

This is useful when a customer claims they can’t access the app or says “someone logged in” — you can quickly sanity-check where and when the last login happened.

The tab also shows a **Delete User Account** section with a **Delete** button.

> **Important:** Deleting a user account is permanent. Only do this when you’re 100% sure the customer wants the account removed.

### Subscriptions

The **Subscriptions** tab helps you understand what the customer currently has.

Based on the UI shown, you may see:

- **Minutes Details** (including “Remaining Minutes”)
- **Active Subscriptions** (for example, it may show “No active subscriptions” if nothing is running)

There is also a **View Billing Details** section with a **View** button that opens the Stripe customer portal to manage billing (when available).

> **Tip:** If “Active Subscriptions” is empty but the customer says they paid, the first thing to verify is whether they’re looking at the correct email/account.

### 1-Min Billing

The **1-Min Billing** tab explains and tracks outbound billing behavior using the “1-minute rule.”

What the UI explains:

- If **50% of the last 100 completed outbound calls** are under **50 seconds**, then outbound calls under **60 seconds** are billed as **1 minute**.

You’ll see summary cards such as:

- **Rule eligible** (Yes/No)
- **Rule applies now** (Yes/No)
- **Avg duration (completed 100)**
- **Success rate (recent outbound)**

There’s also:

- A **Refresh** button
- A toggle for **Minimum 1-minute billing** (an override that applies even if the user meets the rule)
- A “Recent outbound calls (last 100)” table (columns like Created, Status, Duration, Under 50s)

> **Tip:** This tab is especially useful when a customer questions billing for short outbound calls—you can point to how the rule is being applied for their account.

### UTM

The **UTM** tab shows “Marketing UTM Data” captured at signup and starting free trial.

If there’s no attribution, the UI may show a message like “No UTM data available for this user.”

This helps you answer questions like:

- “Did this customer come from a campaign?”
- “Which source drove this signup?” (when data exists)
