## What this page is for

The **Users** page (**User Management**) is where you manage the *customers of your White Label*: the people who buy subscriptions and use your branded app. You’ll find it in the White Label Platform, under **User Management** ([open it here](https://app.callin.io/cms/users)).

From here you can:

- Create new customer accounts
- Search and filter your customers
- Update basic customer details (name and company)
- Review each customer’s login activity, subscriptions, call billing, marketing attribution and Twilio spend cap
- Open the customer’s app directly

At the top of the page you’ll see:

- **Search users…** to find a customer by name or email.
- **Status** to filter by **Active**, **Deleted** or **Banned** customers.
- **Subscriptions** to filter customers by the plan they’re subscribed to.
- **Create User** to add a new customer.

Each row shows the customer’s name, email and company, their status (for example, **Active**), the date they joined (**Joined At**) and four action icons on the right:

| Icon | Action | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pencil | **Edit User** | Change the customer’s name and company. |
| Two squares | **Copy User ID** | Copy the customer’s unique ID, for example to share it with Callin support. |
| Person (orange) | **More User Details** | Open the customer details panel. |
| Link (green) | **Login as this user** | Open the customer’s app as that customer. |

## Create a customer account

When you click **Create User**, the **Create New User** window opens:

- **Email** (required)
- **Password** (required, at least 8 characters)
- **Full Name** (optional)
- **Company** (optional)

<Frame caption="Screenshot: the Create New User window, with example data.">
  <img src="/images/wl-users-create.png" alt="Create New User window with the Email, Password, Full Name and Company fields and the Cancel and Create User buttons" />
</Frame>

Realistic example:

- Email → `sara@northwindclinic.com`
- Password → a secure password you share with the customer through a safe channel
- Full Name → `Sara Ruiz`
- Company → `Northwind Clinic`

Click **Create User**. The customer appears in the list and can sign in to your app with that email and password.

<Tip>
  If you manage many accounts, keeping **Full Name** and **Company** filled in makes search and support much easier.
</Tip>

## Edit basic customer details

Click the pencil icon (**Edit User**) to change:

- **Full Name**
- **Company**

Click **Update User** to save, or **Cancel** to close without changes. The email can’t be changed here.

This is useful when:

- A customer signed up with an incomplete name
- You want the company name to match your internal naming (for example, “Northwind Clinic (Madrid)”)

## Open the customer app with the green icon

The last icon on the right of each row is a **green link icon**: **Login as this user**. It opens your app as that customer, without needing their password.

Use it when you need to:

- Confirm exactly what the customer is seeing
- Check whether a feature is available on their subscription
- Help the customer find a page without guessing

Support example:

<Steps>
  <Step>
    The customer says: “I can’t find my subscriptions.”
  </Step>
  <Step>
    You find them in **Users**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    You click the **green icon** to open their app and guide them with certainty.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  While you’re inside the customer’s app, any change you make applies to their account. Look, help, and leave things as they were unless the customer asked you to change something.
</Warning>

## What you can review inside a customer profile

Click the orange person icon (**More User Details**) to open the customer details panel. At the top you’ll see the customer’s name, email and status. Below, five tabs:

  <img src="/images/wl-users-details.png" alt="Customer details panel on the Info tab with Onboarding (Company), Last Login Details (Device, IP Address, Location, Time), Session history and Security with Delete User Account" />
</Frame>

### Info

- **Onboarding**: the company the customer entered.
- **Last Login Details**: the **Device**, **IP Address**, **Location** and **Time** of their last sign-in.
- **Session history**: their recent sessions.
- **Security**: **Delete User Account** permanently deletes the customer’s account and all its data, after a confirmation.

<Warning>
  Deleting a customer can’t be undone. If you only want to stop their access, check their subscription first.
</Warning>

### Subscriptions

- **Active Subscriptions**: the plans the customer is subscribed to, with their minutes.
- **View Billing Details**: opens the customer’s Stripe customer portal to manage their billing.

### 1-Min Billing

**Outbound billing (1-minute rule)**: if 50% of the customer’s last 100 completed outbound calls are under 50 seconds, their outbound calls under 60 seconds are billed as 1 minute. The tab shows:

- **Rule eligible** and **Rule applies now**, with how many calls were considered and how many were short.
- The **Avg duration** and the **Success rate** of their recent outbound calls.
- **Minimum 1-minute billing**: a switch to apply the 1-minute minimum to this customer even if they don’t meet the rule (**Admin override**).
- **Recent outbound calls (last 100)**: each call with its status, duration and the minutes billed if the rule applies.

<Frame caption="Screenshot: the 1-Min Billing tab. Click Refresh to update the figures.">
  <img src="/images/wl-users-billing.png" alt="1-Min Billing tab with the Outbound billing (1-minute rule) explanation, the Rule eligible, Rule applies now, Avg duration and Success rate boxes, and the Minimum 1-minute billing switch" />

<Tip>
  This is where to look when a customer asks why a short outbound call was billed as a full minute. See also [Telephony](/Automations/Whitelabel/telefhony).
</Tip>

### UTM

**Marketing UTM Data**: the marketing attribution captured when the customer signed up and started their free trial (source, campaign, etc.). If they didn’t arrive through a tracked link, you’ll see **No UTM data available for this user**. See [UTM Analytics](/Automations/Whitelabel/utm).

### Twilio Cap

**Twilio daily spend cap**: turn **Daily cap enforcement** on or off for this customer. When it’s on, the customer takes part in the Twilio daily spend limit, which protects you from unexpected calling costs.

This helps you quickly answer questions like:

- “Did this customer log in recently?”
- “Do they have an active subscription?”
- “Why was a short outbound call billed a certain way?”
- “Did they come from a campaign with UTM parameters?”

<Tip>
  If a customer says “I paid but it’s not active,” the first check is usually confirming they’re using the correct email and account.
</Tip>
