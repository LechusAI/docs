## What is Subaccount Management

Each customer you add to your White Label has their own account: a **subaccount**. **Subaccount Management** lets you review, from one place, the AI agents your customers have built and the calls they make and receive.

You’ll find it at the end of the White Label Platform menu ([open it here](https://app.callin.io/cms/subaccount-management)). It has two tabs: **AI Agents** and **Call History**.

<Tip>
  Use it to help a customer who asks for support: you can see their agent’s setup and their calls without signing in to their account.
</Tip>

## AI Agents

The **AI Agents** tab lists the agents of all your subaccounts.

- Use **All**, **Inbound** and **Outbound** to filter by type of agent.
- Use **Search agents…** to find an agent.
- Click **Refresh** to load the latest changes.

### Agent Details

Open an agent to see its **Agent Details**, in read-only mode:

- **Agent Metadata**: category, template, goal, website, mode, tags and **Assigned Number**.
- The **Greeting Message** and the **Prompt**.
- **Features & Settings**: for example **Call Transfer**, **Max Call Duration**, **Record Calls**, **Voicemail Detection** and **User Speak First**.
- **AI Configuration**: **LLM**, **Temperature**, **Voice**, **Languages** and **Knowledge Base**.
- The agent’s actions, such as **Schedule on Google**, **Transfer Call**, **Send an Email** and **Opt-Out Calls**, and its **Data Extraction**, **Post Call Webhook** and **Retry Configuration**.

### Call minute limit

With **Usage cap**, you can limit how many call minutes an agent can use. This doesn’t change the customer’s subscription billing: it’s an extra limit on top.

The **Call minute limit** window has four tabs:

- **Settings**: turn on **Limit this agent's call minutes** and choose **How should the limit work?**:
  - **Resets every month**: the minutes start again each month. Choose **When does each month start?**.
  - **Fixed pool until expiry**: a set number of minutes until a date. Choose **When should this limit end?**.

  Then set **Maximum minutes allowed** and click **Save settings**.
- **Add minutes**: add more minutes without clearing the usage already counted. If calls were blocked, they work again after you save.
- **Reset usage**: clear the usage back to zero and set a new allowance, for example when a new period starts.
- **History**: every change, with the minutes added or removed and the **Reason** you wrote.

At the top you’ll see the **Usage this period**: minutes left, minutes on calls in progress, the limit type and its dates.

When an agent is close to its limit, you’ll see **Running low**. When it uses all its minutes, you’ll see **Calls blocked**: calls are paused until you add more minutes or reset the limit.

<Warning>
  With **Fixed pool until expiry**, calls also stop when the end date passes. Set a new end date or switch to **Resets every month**.
</Warning>

## Call History

The **Call History** tab shows the calls of all your subaccounts, with the same filters as the agents’ [Call History](/Create-AI-Agent/Call-Actions).

<Frame caption="Screenshot: the Call History tab and its filters.">
  <img src="/images/wl-subaccounts-calls.png" alt="Subaccount Management on the Call History tab with the Export CSV button, the transcript search and the filters Phone Number, Call ID, Agent, Squad, Status, Direction and more" />
</Frame>

- **Search transcripts…** finds calls by what was said.
- Filter by **Phone Number**, **Call ID**, **Agent**, **Squad**, **Status**, **Direction**, **Appointment**, **Call Transfer**, **Voicemail**, **Date Range**, **Call Duration**, **Origin**, **Campaign ID**, **Sentiment** or **Ticket ID**.
- Click **Export CSV** to download the calls you’re viewing.

## Troubleshooting

**Issue:** The list says **No subaccount agents found**.\
**Cause:** Your customers haven’t created agents yet, or the search or filter hides them.\
**Fix:** Clear the search, choose **All** and click **Refresh**.

**Issue:** A customer says their agent stopped calling.\
**Cause:** The agent may have reached its call minute limit (**Calls blocked**) or its end date.\
**Fix:** Open the agent’s **Usage cap** and use **Add minutes**, **Reset usage** or set a new end date.
