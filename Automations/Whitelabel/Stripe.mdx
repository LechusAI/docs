## What this page is for

The **Integrations** page is where you connect your White Label app to the services that make day-to-day operations possible:

- **Payments & subscriptions** with Stripe (so you can charge customers)
- **Voice and SMS** with Twilio (so calling and messaging work)
- **Email sending** with SendGrid (so customers receive important emails)
- **Live chat** with Intercom or Brevo Chat (so customers can contact your team in real time)
- **Marketing tracking** with Google Ads and Google Analytics (so you can measure acquisition and conversions)

You’ll find it in the White Label Platform, under **App Configuration** ([open it here](https://app.callin.io/cms/api-keys)). Use **Search integrations…** to find one quickly.

## Before you connect anything

A few habits make integrations smoother (and safer):

- **Use the right admin account** in each provider (Stripe, Twilio, etc.) so you can access keys and settings.
- **Store keys securely** (treat them like passwords).
- **Limit access** to people who need it. Don’t paste keys into shared docs or chats.

> **Tip:** If an integration doesn’t work immediately, double-check for extra spaces at the beginning or end of a key before trying anything else.

## How connecting an integration works

1. Click an integration card. A side panel opens.
2. Read the blue box at the top: it explains where to find each value.
3. For **Stripe**, click **Connect** and sign in to Stripe. For the others, copy the values from the provider’s dashboard and paste them into the fields (the ones marked with `*` are required).
4. Click **Save**.

To close a panel without saving, click the **X** in the top-right corner.

## Stripe

**What it’s for:** Stripe is used for **payment processing**: accepting payments, charging customers, managing subscriptions and receiving payouts. It’s what lets you run a real subscription business.

**When you need it:** If you plan to create paid plans and charge customers, Stripe is one of the first integrations to set up.

### How to connect Stripe

1. Open the **Stripe** card. The **Connect with Stripe** panel opens.
2. Click **Connect**.
3. Sign in to your Stripe account, or create a new one when prompted.
4. When you finish, you’re redirected back to Callin and your account is linked automatically.

> **Important:** Connect the Stripe account where you want to receive your customers’ payments. If you use a different Stripe account later, connect it again from this card.

## Twilio

**What it’s for:** Twilio powers **voice calls and SMS**, and phone number management.

**When you need it:** If your White Label includes inbound or outbound calls or SMS, Twilio is essential. That’s why it’s marked **Required**.

### What you’ll need (as shown in the panel)

- **Account SID**\*
- **Auth Token**\*

### How to connect Twilio

1. Open the **Twilio** card. The **Add Twilio API Keys** panel opens.
2. From your Twilio dashboard, copy your **Account SID** and **Auth Token**.
3. Paste them into the matching fields. Click the eye icon to check the token you pasted.
4. Click **Save**.

> **Tip:** If calls or SMS stop working later, Twilio is the first integration to re-check.

## SendGrid

**What it’s for:** SendGrid **sends the emails** of your White Label app: automated emails and notifications your customers rely on.

**When you need it:** If your customers should receive emails from your branded app, set up SendGrid early. It’s marked **Required**.

### What you’ll need (as shown in the panel)

- **API Key**\*
- **Sender Email**\* (a sender email verified in SendGrid)

1. Open the **SendGrid** card. The **Add SendGrid API Keys** panel opens.
2. Copy your **API Key** from SendGrid.
3. Enter a **Sender Email** that is verified in SendGrid.
4. Click **Save**.

> **Common mistake:** Using a sender email that isn’t verified in SendGrid. If emails don’t send, verify the sender first.

## Intercom

**What it’s for:** Intercom adds **live chat and customer support** to your customers’ dashboard, so they can message your team without leaving the app.

**When you need it:** If support and onboarding are part of what you sell. It’s optional.

### What you’ll need (as shown in the panel)

- **App ID**\* (from your Intercom workspace settings)

## Analytics & Tracking API Keys

**What it’s for:** This integration connects your White Label to **marketing and analytics tracking**:

- **Google Ads** conversions, with a Pixel ID
- **Google Analytics (GA4)**, with a Measurement ID

**Why this matters for White Label:** If you run paid campaigns, you want traffic and conversions tracked for your brand, so you can measure performance and ROI. Both fields are optional: fill in only the ones you use.

### What you’ll need (as shown in the panel)

- **Google Ads Pixel ID** (format: `AW-123456789`)
- **Google Analytics Measurement ID (GA4)** (format: `G-XXXXXXXXXX`)

### How to connect tracking

1. Open **Analytics & Tracking API Keys**.
2. In Google Ads, go to **Tools & Settings → Conversions**, create or open a website conversion action and copy the Conversion ID (it looks like `AW-123456789`). Paste it into **Google Ads Pixel ID**.
3. In Google Analytics (GA4), go to **Admin → Data Streams → Web**, select your website stream and copy the Measurement ID (it looks like `G-XXXXXXXXXX`). Paste it into **Google Analytics Measurement ID (GA4)**.
4. Click **Save**.

### What happens after you save (as described in the panel)

- For your brand only, the app automatically adds the Google Ads and GA4 scripts using the IDs you entered.
- Traffic and conversions are tracked separately for each White Label brand.
- If a field is left empty, that provider’s script isn’t loaded for your brand.
- Only admins and brand owners with access to the White Label Platform can view or change these IDs.

> **Tip:** Connect tracking before your first campaigns, so you don’t lose attribution data.
