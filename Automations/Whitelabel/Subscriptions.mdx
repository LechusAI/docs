## What this page is for

The **Subscriptions** page gives you an overview of all your customers’ subscriptions. You’ll find it in the White Label Platform, under **Plans & Billing** ([open it here](https://app.callin.io/cms/subscriptions)).

It’s designed for reviewing, so you can quickly answer questions like:

- “Which plan is this customer on?”
- “Is their subscription active, in trial, past due, or canceled?”
- “How many minutes are included and how many have been used?”
- “When did the subscription start and when does it end?”

> **Tip:** Subscriptions appear here when your customers subscribe to one of your [Subscription Plans](/Automations/Whitelabel/SubscriptionsPlans) and pay through Stripe. Until then, you’ll see **No subscriptions**.

## How to use the list

### Search subscriptions

At the top, use the **Search subscriptions…** field to find a customer quickly (for example, by email or name). If nothing matches, you’ll see **No subscriptions found**: try a different search.

### Understand the columns

Each row is one subscription:

- **User**: the customer (email or name).
- **Plan**: the plan name and its type: **Monthly**, **Annual**, **One Time** or **Pay-as-you-go**. Plans in a free trial show a **Trial** badge.
- **Price**: the cost of the plan.
- **Minutes**: the minutes included and how many have been **Used**.
- **Status**: the current state of the subscription (see below).
- **Start Date** and **End Date**: the subscription period.

From each row you can also **Copy Subscription ID**, for example to find the payment in Stripe or to share it with Callin support.

## Subscription status examples

The list can show these statuses:

- **Active**: the subscription is running and paid.
- **Trialing**: the customer is in the free trial period.
- **Past Due**: the last payment failed and Stripe is retrying it.
- **Unpaid**: the payment wasn’t collected after the retries.
- **Canceled**: the subscription was canceled.

> **Important:** If a customer says “I paid” but their status isn’t **Active**, first check that they’re using the right email and account. Then look at their status here and at their billing in [Users](/Automations/Whitelabel/Clients) → **Subscriptions** → **View Billing Details**.
