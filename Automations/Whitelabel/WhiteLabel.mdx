## What “White Label” means in Callin

A **White Label** in Callin is designed for teams (especially **agencies**) who want to **resell Callin’s technology under their own brand**.

Instead of sending your customers to “Callin,” you present the experience as *your product*: your name, your domain, your look and feel, and your billing model. Callin runs the infrastructure underneath.

> **Tip:** Think of it as “Callin on the inside, your brand on the outside.”

## Where you access White Label

Everything for your White Label is managed in the **White Label Platform**. There are two ways to open it:

- Click **Console** in the top-right corner of the app.
- Or click the platform selector at the top of the left sidebar and choose **White Label Platform** (“Branding, billing, and customer management”).

It opens on **White Label Configuration**. To return to your agents, click **Back to App** in the top-right corner.

To go, click here: [Open White Label Configuration](https://app.callin.io/cms/white-label-configuration)

## What you can do as an agency with White Label

White Label is built to help you operate like a product company, even if you’re an agency.

From the White Label Platform, you can:

- **Offer a branded platform** to your customers (your name and presentation, not “Callin”).
- **Use your own domain** so customers feel like they’re inside your ecosystem.
- **Create subscription plans** you sell to customers (for example, “Starter,” “Pro,” “Enterprise”).
- **Charge customers through Stripe** (when connected) so billing happens under your workflow.
- **Manage customers and team members** from one place, without juggling multiple tools.

### A realistic example

Imagine you run an agency called **Northwind Voice Studio**. You want to sell an “AI receptionist” product to local clinics.

- Your customer sees the platform branded as **Northwind Reception**.
- They access it through a domain like **app.northwindreception.com**.
- You sell them a **monthly plan** you created in the White Label Platform.
- Payments run through **Stripe**, and you manage the customer’s access from **Users**.

### App Configuration

- **White Label Configuration**: your app name, custom domain, currency, language, server location, legal pages, and your brand’s colors and logos. See [White Label Configuration](/Automations/Whitelabel/User).
- **Integrations**: connect third-party services such as Stripe for payments. See [Integrations](/Automations/Whitelabel/Stripe).
- **Telephony**: set up the phone numbers and calling your customers use. See [Telephony](/Automations/Whitelabel/telefhony).

> **Tip:** If billing is part of your business model, connect Stripe early so you can test your full customer flow.

### User Management

- **Users**: search, create, and manage your customers and team members. See [Users](/Automations/Whitelabel/Clients).

### Plans & Billing

- **Subscriptions**: see who is subscribed and to which plan. See [Subscriptions](/Automations/Whitelabel/Subscriptions).
- **Subscription Plans**: create the plans you sell, with their price and billing period. See [Subscription Plans](/Automations/Whitelabel/SubscriptionsPlans).
- **Features & Capabilities**: choose what each plan includes. See [Features & Capabilities](/Automations/Whitelabel/Features).

To understand how Callin charges you and how you set your own prices, see [Billing and Fees](/Billing).

### Analytics & Marketing

- **UTM Analytics**: see which sources and campaigns brought you sign-ups. See [UTM Analytics](/Automations/Whitelabel/utm).

### Content Management

- **Guides**, **FAQs** and **Agent Templates**: the help content and ready-made agents your customers see in your app. See [Guides](/Automations/Whitelabel/Guide), [FAQs](/Automations/Whitelabel/Faqs) and [Agent Templates](/Automations/Whitelabel/Agent-templates).
- **Subaccount Management**: review your customers’ agents and calls, and limit each agent’s call minutes. See [Subaccount Management](/Automations/Whitelabel/Subaccounts).

## Recommended setup order (so you don’t miss anything)

When launching a White Label for the first time, this order keeps things smooth:

1. Set your **App Name**, **Currency**, and **App Language** in **White Label Configuration**.
2. Add your **Custom Domain** and complete the **DNS Configuration**.
3. Upload your logos and set your colors in the **Appearance** tab.
4. Connect critical **Integrations** (especially Stripe if you will bill customers).
5. Create your **Subscription Plans** and choose their **Features & Capabilities**.
6. Add and manage customers and teammates in **Users**.
7. Monitor sign-ups and channels in **UTM Analytics**.
