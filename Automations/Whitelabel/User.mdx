## What you’ll find on this page

**White Label Configuration** is the control center for your branded app. It’s the first section of the White Label Platform, under **App Configuration**. At the top, you’ll see two tabs:

- **Configuration**: core settings that affect how your app works (name, domain, billing defaults, legal pages and the minutes warning email).
- **Appearance**: the visual identity your customers will see (colors, email language, logos, favicon and default theme).

Open it here: [Open White Label Configuration](https://app.callin.io/cms/white-label-configuration)

## Configuration

### App Name

**App Name** is shown in the sidebar, page titles, and emails. It’s the name your customers will recognize.

A good rule: use the same name you use in your sales materials.\
Example: if you sell “Northwind Reception,” set the App Name to **Northwind Reception**.

### Custom Domain (recommended: use a subdomain)

In **Custom Domain**, enter the domain customers will use to access the app, without `http://` or `https://`.

Callin recommends using a **subdomain you control**, for example:

- `app.yourdomain.com`

Why a subdomain matters:

- Your **root domain** (like `yourdomain.com`) typically hosts your marketing website, landing pages, blog, and tracking scripts.
- Keeping the product on `app.yourdomain.com` helps avoid conflicts and keeps your website setup independent.

> **Tip:** Pick a simple subdomain your customers will remember: `app`, `portal`, or `dashboard`.

### DNS Configuration

After you enter your custom domain, click **Save**, then click **DNS Configuration**. Until the domain is saved, you’ll see the message **Please save your domain first before installing SSL or configuring DNS**.

**DNS Configuration** opens a side panel with two ways to set up DNS:

- **Provider** (automatic-assisted)
- **Manual** (you add the records yourself)

#### Provider (automatic-assisted setup)

Use **Provider** when your domain is managed by **Cloudflare**, **GoDaddy** or **Namecheap**. You select your provider and Callin helps you set up the domain automatically.

What you do:

1. Open the **Provider** tab.
2. In **DNS Provider**, select your provider: **Cloudflare**, **GoDaddy** or **Namecheap**.
3. Click **Discover**.

What to expect:

- Callin will try to guide you through the DNS setup for the domain you entered.
- If something fails, you’ll see **Something went wrong. Please check your configuration and DNS records and try after some time.** That usually means the DNS records don’t match what’s expected yet. Wait a little, or use **Manual**.

#### Manual (add DNS records yourself)

Use **Manual** if:

- Your provider is not listed, or
- Provider setup fails, or
- You prefer to manage DNS yourself.

On the **Manual** tab, Callin shows **Add these DNS records at your registrar** and a table with the records for your domain. For example, for `app.northwindreception.com`:

| Type | Name | Value | TTL |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | `@` | the IP address shown in your panel | 300 or auto |
| CNAME | `app` | `northwindreception.com` | 300 or auto |

The table includes:

- **Type** (for example, A or CNAME)
- **Name** (for example, `@` or `app`)
- **Value** (the destination)
- **TTL** (often “300 or auto” depending on your provider)

Your workflow looks like this:

1. Open **Manual**.
2. Add each DNS record exactly as shown in the table.
3. When the records are in place, click **Install SSL** to finish.

### Currency

**Currency** is used for plan prices and billing. You can choose **USD - US Dollar**, **AUD - Australian Dollar**, **CAD - Canadian Dollar**, **EUR - Euro**, **GBP - British Pound Sterling** or **BRL - Brazilian Real**. Type in the search box to find one quickly.

Set it to match how you plan to charge customers. This is especially important if you will create **subscription plans** and charge through Stripe, because customers expect consistency between pricing and billing.

### App Language

**App Language** sets the default language of the app for your customers. You can choose English, Español, Italiano, Português, हिन्दी (Hindi), Deutsch, Nederlands, Français or Português (Brasil).

If you serve customers in several countries, choose the language of your main audience.

### Server location

**Server location** lets you choose the geographic region where your data is hosted, to meet data residency and compliance requirements. You can choose **USA – Ashburn (Virginia)** or **Germany – Frankfurt**.

If you work with customers that have strict requirements (healthcare, finance, public sector), align this setting with what you promised contractually.

### Privacy Policy and Terms of Use

These fields link to your legal pages:

- **Privacy Policy**: where you explain how you handle personal data.
- **Terms of Use**: the rules customers agree to when using your service.

Paste the full URL of each page, for example `https://yourdomain.com/privacy`.

> **Tip:** Even if you’re an agency, treat this like a product: publish these pages on your main website (root domain) and link them here.

### Minutes exhausted warning email

**Minutes exhausted warning email** warns your customers by email when their included minutes have been fully used.

1. Turn on **Enable warning email**. The email editor opens.
2. Write the **Subject**.
3. Choose the **Body type**: **Normal text** (plain text; line breaks are kept) or **HTML** (raw HTML).
4. Write the **Body**. By default it includes your app name and your domain.
5. Click **Preview email** to see the final email your customers will receive, then **Close**.

To edit it later, click **Configure email**. Remember to click **Save**.

### Colors

Click a color square to pick a color:

- **Primary Color**: used for buttons and highlights in the app.
- **Secondary Color**: used for accents and states.
- **Email Primary Color**: the main accent color in your transactional emails.
- **Email CTA Button Color**: the color of primary buttons and links inside your emails.

> **Tip:** If your brand already has a style guide, start with your primary brand color, then pick a secondary color that looks good on both light and dark backgrounds.

### Email Language

**Email Language** is the default language of the emails your customers receive. It uses the same languages as **App Language**.

### Logos (light and dark)

Upload two versions of your logo:

- **Logo** (**Select logo**): for light backgrounds.
- **Dark Logo** (**Select dark logo**): for dark backgrounds.

Use PNG or SVG, up to 2 MB, ideally with a transparent background. The same logo isn’t always readable on both themes.

> **Important:** If your logo appears tiny, the image usually has a large white margin around it: Callin scales the whole image down to fit. A tightly cropped, transparent PNG usually solves it.

### Favicon

The **Favicon** is the small icon shown in the browser tab and bookmarks. Click **Select favicon** and upload an ICO or PNG of 32×32 or 48×48 pixels, up to 1 MB.

### Default Theme

**Default Theme** controls how the app looks by default:

- **System**: follows each user’s device theme.
- **Light**: light mode.
- **Dark**: dark mode.

A practical approach: choose **System** if you want the app to feel native to each user’s preferences.

When you’re done, click **Save**.
