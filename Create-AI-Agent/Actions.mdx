## Actions

Actions is the place where you connect Callin to other platforms so your AI Agents can **schedule**, **sync customer data**, **trigger automations**, and **send updates**—without doing everything manually. In the app, the page is called **Integrations**.

[Open Actions in the Console](https://app.callin.io/actions)

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Open your Callin Console.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    In the left menu, go to **Integrations → Actions**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

At the top of the page you’ll find:

- **Search integrations**: type a name (for example, “Google”, “Slack” or “Zapier”).
- **Category filters**: **All**, **Calendar**, **CRM**, **Automation**, **Communication**, **AI** and **Custom**.
- **Request integration**: ask for a platform that isn’t on the list yet.

## How connecting works

Each integration card shows its category, a short description and a status or button:

- **Connect** → The integration isn’t connected yet. Click it to start.
- **Connected** with **Manage** → The integration is already connected (example: Google Calendar).
- **Coming soon** → The integration is listed but not available to connect yet (example: WhatsApp and Slack).
- **New** → A recently added integration (example: Claude).
- Some platforms use a different button label (example: **Go to n8n**).

Click a connected card to see its details: what it does, its features, and the **View** and **Disconnect** buttons. You’ll also find **Disconnect** in the **⋮** menu at the top right of the card.

<Frame caption="Screenshot: The details of a connected integration.">
  <img src="/images/actions-details.png" alt="The details window of the Google Calendar integration with its description, the list of features, the Connected status and the View and Disconnect buttons." />
</Frame>

General flow:

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Click the integration card’s button (**Connect**, **Manage** or **Go to n8n**).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Follow the setup steps. Depending on the platform, Callin opens the platform’s sign-in page in a new tab (for example, GoHighLevel or Zapier) or a setup page inside Callin (for example, Claude, Cal.com or Custom Integration).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Return to Callin and confirm the card status updates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The exact authorization screens can vary depending on your account, plan, or how your organization manages permissions.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  **Common mistake:** Connecting the wrong account (for example, the wrong Google account) and later wondering why events or data appear in the wrong place.
</Warning>

### Claude (New)

**What it’s for**<br />Work with your AI agents, calls and transcripts from Claude, using MCP. Claude can only access the Callin data you authorize.

**What you can do**

- View your AI agents
- Review recent calls
- Access call transcripts
- Summarize conversations
- Search Callin calls using natural language

**How to connect in Callin**

<Steps>
  <Step>
    On the **Claude** card, click **Connect**. The **Claude × Callin** page opens.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click **Connect Claude** to open Claude.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    In Claude, open **Customize → Connectors** and add a custom connector.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Paste the **Callin connector URL** shown on the page (use **Copy URL**).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Sign in to Callin if asked, then click **Allow access** on the consent screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

No API keys are pasted: authorization happens securely in Callin.

**Example questions to ask Claude**

- “Show my latest 10 calls.”
- “Summarize my latest customer call.”
- “Find calls handled by my sales agent this week.”

### Google Calendar

**What it’s for**<br />Sync availability and let your AI agent book appointments directly on Google Calendar, the calendar your team already uses.

**How to connect in Callin**

- If it’s not connected, click **Connect** on the Google Calendar card and complete the Google authorization steps.
- If it’s already connected, you’ll see **Connected** and **Manage**. Click the card to see its details, with:
  - **View** to review the connection
  - **Disconnect** to remove the connection

For the full setup, see [Google Calendar](/Create-AI-Agent/Google-Calendar).

**Example uses with AI Agents**

- A scheduling agent can check availability in real time and book confirmed appointments in your calendar (for example: “Demo with Alex Rivera — Tue 3:30 PM”).
- An agent can help reduce no-shows by using calendar details as the “source of truth” for upcoming meetings (for example, confirming: “You’re booked for Wednesday at 10:00 AM”).

### Cal.com

**What it’s for**<br />Cal.com is an appointment scheduling platform. Connect it so your AI agent can check availability and book meetings automatically.

**How to connect in Callin**

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Find **Cal.com** and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    On the **Cal.com Integration** page, paste your API key in **Connect with API Key**. You’ll find it in your Cal.com settings (there’s a link on the page).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click **Connect**, then return to Actions and confirm the card shows **Connected**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Example uses with AI Agents**

- A booking agent can guide a customer to the right appointment type (for example: “15-minute intro call” vs. “30-minute onboarding”).
- If your team uses Cal.com scheduling links, an agent can help route customers to the right option based on intent (sales, support, onboarding).

- A sales agent can use what it learned during a call to support better handoffs (for example: “Interested in the Pro plan, wants pricing by email”).

**What it’s for**<br />n8n is a workflow automation platform. Build automated workflows that trigger from call events and send data to your apps.

**Example uses with AI Agents**

### Custom Integration

**What it’s for**<br />Build your own integration with the Callin API to send and receive call data. It’s for teams that want to connect Callin to their own systems.

**How to connect in Callin**

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Click **Connect** on the **Custom Integration** card. The **Receive Data (Make Call)** page opens, with two tabs: **AI Workflows** and **Logs**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click **Create Workflow** and fill in:
    - **Workflow Name**
    - **AI Agent**: the agent that will make the call.
    - **Mandatory Fields**: the data your request must include. By default: **name**, **phone** and **email**, each with a definition. Use **Add Entity** to add more (for example, `firstName`, `appointmentDate` or `isInterested`).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click **Save**. Each workflow gets its own unique **Webhook URL**: copy it and use it to send data from your external services. Make sure to include the mandatory fields in your request.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Check the **Logs** tab to see the requests received.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  <img src="/images/actions-custom.png" alt="The Create Workflow window with Workflow Name, AI Agent, the Mandatory Fields table (name, phone, email) with their definitions, and the Add Entity button." />

**Example uses with AI Agents**

- When a new lead fills in a form on your website, send it to the workflow’s Webhook URL so the agent calls them right away.
- Trigger calls from your own backend or CRM whenever a contact reaches a specific stage.

<Tip>
  Custom Integration is a good choice when you already have an internal tool and want it to trigger Callin calls automatically.
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

### The integration doesn’t show “Connected”

- Refresh the page and check again.
- Try disconnecting and reconnecting (if the option exists).
- Confirm you signed in with the correct account on the provider side.

### You connected the wrong account

- Use **Disconnect** (on the card’s **⋮** menu or in its details), then reconnect and select the correct account during authorization.

### Buttons or options look different

Some labels and setup steps can vary based on your plan, your organization settings, or provider changes.
