## Overview
In Callin.io, phone numbers are the foundation of your AI communication system. Without at least one active number, your AI agent can’t make or receive calls.

You can add numbers in four ways:
- **Purchase** a number in Callin.io (Twilio-managed)
- **Import from Twilio** (if you already have numbers there)
- **Import from Telnyx** (after connecting Telnyx)
- **Connect BYOC / SIP trunk** (Bring Your Own Carrier)

This guide explains each option, how **Callin compute minutes** work, and the exact setup steps.

---

## Before You Start
- You need **at least one active number** to make or receive calls.
- Carrier telephony costs (Twilio, Telnyx, or SIP) are billed **by your provider**, not inside Callin.io.

---

## How Minutes and Billing Work in Callin
Callin tracks a single usage counter:

### Callin Minutes (Compute Minutes)
Compute minutes represent the time your AI agent is active: **listening, processing, and responding**.

Callin does **not** maintain a second “call minutes” counter.

### Test Call
- Consumes **Callin compute minutes only**.

### Real Calls (Inbound/Outbound)
- Consume **Callin compute minutes** while the agent is active.
- Carrier telephony costs depend on your provider and do **not** appear as a second minutes counter in Callin.

---

## Billing Rules for Outbound Calls Made in the App (Rounding Rule)

### Default Billing
- Outbound calls are billed **per second**.

### Short-Call Rounding Rule
If **50% or more** of your **last 100 outbound calls** made in the app have a duration **under 50 seconds**, then from that point forward Callin will bill the **first 60 seconds** of each outbound call (or any fraction of it) as **one full minute** (rounded up to 1 minute).

### Scope of the Rule (Very Important)
This rounding rule applies **only** when outbound calls in the app are made using:
- **Personal Number**
- **Numbers purchased directly through Callin.io** (Twilio-managed)

This rounding rule **does not apply** to:
- **Numbers imported from Twilio**
- **Numbers imported from Telnyx**
- **BYOC / SIP trunking numbers**

## Understanding Your Options

### 1) Twilio
Twilio is a cloud communications platform that offers APIs for calls, messaging, and video.

Choose **Twilio** if you want:
- A quick, low-maintenance setup
- Built-in reliability with minimal configuration
- Global coverage with easy number search and purchase

When you buy or import a Twilio number, Callin.io connects it automatically for use within the platform. You don’t need to manage SIP routing manually.

### 2) Telnyx
Telnyx is a communications provider that offers deeper control over routing and SIP trunk configuration.

Choose **Telnyx** if you want:
- Full control over SIP trunks and routing
- Advanced settings (codecs, encryption, regional routing)
- A flexible setup for compliance or enterprise telephony

Telnyx requires a slightly more technical setup, but becomes highly customizable once connected.

### 3) SIP Trunking (BYOC)
SIP trunking lets you connect your own telecom infrastructure directly to Callin.io using **BYOC (Bring Your Own Carrier)**.

Choose **BYOC** if:
- You already have an existing SIP carrier (PBX/corporate system)
- You need to manage routing, SBCs, or regional carriers
- You want Callin.io as a voice automation layer over your existing telephony

With BYOC, you manually configure SIP credentials and routes. This is designed for advanced users or telecom teams.

## Steps

### 1) Connect Your Provider Through BYOC
Go to **Telephony → BYOC** to link Telnyx or another SIP provider.

#### If You Use Telnyx
1. Select the **Telnyx** tab.
2. Enter your **API Key**.
3. Callin.io retrieves your active numbers (DIDs).
4. Select the numbers you want to activate.
5. Callin.io sets up inbound and outbound SIP trunking automatically.
6. Click **Start** to begin synchronization.
7. Once complete, your Telnyx numbers appear in your account.

#### If You Use Another SIP Provider
1. Open **Other SIP Provider** tab.
2. Configure your SIP trunk in three sections:

**Basic**
- Set a name
- Enter your number in **E.164** format

**Inbound**
- Define where inbound calls will be sent (your **SBC/FQDN**)
- Choose **TLS** or **UDP** transport
- Enable **media encryption** if supported

**Outbound**
- Enter SIP credentials (**SBC address**, **username**, **password**)
- Select outbound country permissions

3. Click **Save & Import**.

> **Note:** If the SBC address or credentials are wrong, the trunk won’t connect.

---

### 2) Add Numbers to Your Account
Go to **Telephony → Numbers**. This section lists all numbers associated with your account.

Click **+ Add Number**, then choose one option:

#### Option A) Purchase Number (Twilio-Managed)
Purchase a number directly through Callin.io (Twilio-managed).

1. Choose the country.
2. Choose the type (**local** or **toll-free**).
3. Search by area code if needed.
4. Select a number and purchase it.

**Rounding rule reminder:**  
Outbound calls in the app using a **Callin-purchased** number may trigger the short-call rounding rule.

---

#### Option B) Import From Twilio
Import existing numbers from your Twilio account.

1. Enter your **Account SID** and **Auth Token**.
2. Select the numbers to import.
3. Callin.io links them automatically.
4. Assign them to AI agents to start inbound/outbound calls.

**Rounding rule reminder:**  
Numbers imported from **Twilio** are **not** within the scope of the short-call rounding rule.

---

#### Option C) Add Personal Number (Outbound-Only)
Add your personal number (e.g., your mobile phone) to place calls from the app.

**Important limitation:** Personal numbers are **outbound-only** and cannot receive inbound calls in the app.

**Verification steps**
1. Enter your phone number.
2. You’ll receive a verification call.
3. Enter the code when prompted.
4. Once verified, your number is added.

**Rounding rule reminder:**  
Outbound calls in the app using **Personal Number** may trigger the short-call rounding rule.

---

#### Option D) Add Telnyx Number
If your Telnyx account is already connected, import your active Telnyx numbers.

1. Select the numbers you want.
2. Click **Import**.

Telnyx supports advanced control such as:
- Managing routing logic
- Adjusting codecs and audio encryption
- Enabling multi-region redundancy

**Rounding rule reminder:**  
Numbers imported from **Telnyx** are **not** within the scope of the short-call rounding rule.

---

### 3) Verify Your Numbers
Each number card shows its type and provider.

From **Telephony → Numbers**, verify:
- Renewal date
- Connection type (Direct, SIP, Twilio, or Telnyx)
- Activation status

Make sure all numbers are **active** and properly linked.

---

## Tips & Notes
- If you’re starting fast, use **Twilio** (purchase inside Callin.io).
- If you need routing and encryption control, use **Telnyx** or **BYOC**.
- Carrier charges are billed by your provider; Callin tracks only **compute minutes**.

---

## Troubleshooting

**Symptom:** Can’t make calls  
**Likely cause:** Number is inactive or not connected to a valid provider  
**Fix:** Confirm status in **Telephony → Numbers** and validate your provider connection in **Telephony → BYOC**.

**Symptom:** Personal number not receiving calls  
**Likely cause:** Personal numbers are outbound-only  
**Fix:** Use a Twilio/Telnyx/BYOC number for inbound calling.

**Symptom:** Missing imported numbers  
**Likely cause:** Wrong credentials or DID permissions  
**Fix:** Re-check API keys/tokens and confirm the numbers are active in Twilio/Telnyx.
