---

You don't need an Italian number to start. Keep the number your customers already know, forward it to Callin.io, and request an Italian number when you're ready.

## Step 1: Forward your current number

Your number stays with your current carrier. You only need a number in Callin.io to forward your calls to.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    In **Numbers**, buy a number. US and Canada numbers are active right away.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Ask your carrier how to forward your calls to that number. Don't turn forwarding on until you've tested the agent (Step 3).
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [How to connect your number to Callin](/Create-AI-Agent/ConnectNumber) for the details.

<Warning>
  **Common mistake:** Forwarding your calls to a **Personal Number**. Personal Numbers are outbound-only, so nobody answers.
</Warning>

## Step 2: Create your first agent

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Go to **AI Agents** and click **Create Agent**. See [AI Agents](/Create-AI-Agent/Create-Agent-Enchanced).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Set **Language** to Italian and the time zone to Rome.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Set **Call Direction** to **Inbound** or **Both**, so the agent answers incoming calls.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Assign the number from Step 1 to the agent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 3: Test the agent

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Click **Test AI agent** at the top right and talk to the agent from your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Call the agent's number and check that it answers as you expect.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    When you're happy with it, turn forwarding on with your carrier.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 4: Request an Italian number

Italian numbers aren't immediate: you request them in **Numbers** and send the documents they ask for. See [Numbers And BYOC](/Create-AI-Agent/Configure-Numbers).

<Note>
  With a monthly plan, one single number is free, with setup and monthly fee included. With pay as you go, it's charged separately.
</Note>

Once the number is active, assign it to your agent.
