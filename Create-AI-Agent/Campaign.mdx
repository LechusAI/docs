## Overview
Campaigns in **Callin.io** allow you to automate outbound communication at scale. They combine AI agents, contact lists, and scheduling logic so your agents can handle large volumes of calls naturally and efficiently.

A campaign consists of three key elements:

- **AI Agent** – the configured voice that executes the calls.
- **Contact List** – drawn from your [Contacts](https://app.callin.io/contacts) section or imported via CSV.
- **Execution Plan** – determines when and how the campaign runs (immediate launch or scheduled window).

By combining these components, you can create large-scale, personalized outreach operations — from lead generation and reminders to surveys and customer follow-ups.

## Before You Start
Campaigns depend on data and configuration from other parts of your Callin.io workspace:

- Ensure your [Contacts](https://app.callin.io/contacts) are properly uploaded and validated.
- Have at least one **AI agent** ready with a tested prompt and voice setup.
- Verify your time zone and scheduling preferences for accurate campaign timing.

Each campaign is capable of calling hundreds or thousands of contacts, but success depends on well-prepared data and clear AI conversation flows.

## Steps

### 1. Access Campaigns
Go to [Campaigns](https://app.callin.io/campaigns) in the left sidebar. You’ll find two main tabs:

- **Campaigns:** lists all active, scheduled, and completed campaigns.
- **Campaigns Analytics:** shows visual reports with key metrics like total calls, call duration, and appointments.

Each campaign card includes:
- Campaign name
- Assigned AI agent
- Current status (e.g., *Completed*, *In Progress*, *Scheduled*)

Click **View** to inspect details or **+ Create Campaign** to build a new one.

### 2. Create a New Campaign
Click **+ Create Campaign**, then complete the setup form:

- **Campaign Name:** Choose a clear and descriptive title.
- **AI Agent:** Select the agent who will make the calls.
- **Select Contacts:** Choose an existing contact list or upload a new CSV file.

If you upload a file, make sure to:
1. Map your columns correctly for **Phone**, **Name**, and **Email**.
2. Assign a default country if your phone numbers lack the `+` prefix.
3. Validate the data preview before confirming the import.

Once imported or selected, your contact list becomes part of the campaign configuration.

### 3. Select and Manage Contacts
After setting up the basics, review your contact list within the campaign editor:

- Check the box next to each contact you want to include.
- Use stored data fields such as **name**, **email**, or **phone number** in your AI agent’s prompt via dynamic variables like `{{name}}` or `{{email}}`.

Example:
```
Hi {{name}}, I’m calling to confirm your appointment tomorrow at {{hour}}.
```

Each variable dynamically pulls the real data from your contacts, allowing for personalized AI calls without manual editing.

### 4. Choose How to Launch
You have two ways to start your campaign:

- **Launch Now:** The campaign begins immediately. The AI agent will start contacting selected contacts sequentially.
- **Schedule for Later:** Choose a specific start and end time, and define the time zone.

Scheduling is ideal for time-sensitive operations — for example, ensuring calls are placed during local business hours or specific follow-up periods.

After configuring your schedule, click **Launch** or **Schedule** to confirm.

### 5. Analyze Performance
Once your campaign is running or completed, open the **Campaigns Analytics** tab to monitor key metrics. You’ll see:

- **Total Appointments:** Number of meetings successfully scheduled.
- **Total Calls:** The overall number of calls made by your agents.
- **Average Call Duration:** The mean length of conversations.
- **AMD Detection Rate:** Percentage of calls that reached an answering machine.

You’ll also find graphical reports, such as:

- **Connected Calls Volume Over Time** – helps identify engagement peaks.
- **Appointments by Hour** – visualizes when conversions occur.
- **Call Outcomes & Key Metrics** – shows distribution between completed, missed, or failed calls.

These analytics are refreshed automatically after each campaign completes, allowing you to measure effectiveness and refine your strategy.

### 6. Why Campaigns Matter
Campaigns allow you to combine automation, personalization, and scalability within one workflow. They provide:

- **Scale:** Reach hundreds or thousands of people automatically.
- **Automation:** Eliminate manual dialing; let AI handle repetitive communication.
- **Personalization:** Each call sounds unique, referencing contact-specific data.
- **Analytics:** Gain actionable insights from every call and conversion.

Use campaigns to automate follow-ups, reminders, lead generation, or customer feedback collection — all while maintaining human-like engagement.

## Troubleshooting

### No Contacts Available
- **Cause:** No contacts have been uploaded or selected.
- **Fix:** Go to [Contacts](https://app.callin.io/contacts) and import or add contacts before starting your campaign.

### Campaign Won’t Start
- **Cause:** Missing configuration, such as no agent assigned or no contacts selected.
- **Fix:** Ensure both an AI agent and at least one contact are included before launching.

### No Analytics Data
- **Cause:** Campaign data is still processing or hasn’t completed.
- **Fix:** Wait a few minutes after completion; analytics refresh automatically once results are finalized.

### Incorrect Timezone or Schedule
- **Cause:** Campaign timezone not set properly.
- **Fix:** Double-check the timezone settings before scheduling to ensure local time accuracy.
