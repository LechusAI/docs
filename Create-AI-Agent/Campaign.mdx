## Overview

In Callin.io, **Campaigns** let your AI Agents take real action at scale.\
They’re how your virtual team makes outbound calls, reminds customers, follows up on leads, and gathers feedback — all automatically.

You design the mission once: decide **who** to call, **what** to say, and **when** to do it.\
From that point, your AI Agent handles everything while you watch progress unfold in real time.

You can view and manage your campaigns directly from the [Campaigns](https://app.callin.io/campaigns) page.

---

## Why Campaigns Matter

Campaigns are designed to save time while keeping the **human touch**.\
They’re useful for situations like:

- **Appointment reminders:** “Hi Emma, just confirming your appointment for tomorrow at 10 AM.”
- **Lead follow-ups:** “Hi Alex, would you like to book your demo this week?”
- **Customer surveys:** “Press 1 if you’re happy with your last delivery.”
- **Mass updates:** “We’ve just released new features — here’s what’s new.”

Instead of having humans manually dial every number, you train your AI once and it scales that tone and logic across every conversation.

## How Campaigns Connect with Contacts and Agents

Every campaign depends on two key parts:

1. **Contacts** – the people your AI will reach.\
   You can manage them from the [Contacts](https://app.callin.io/contacts) section: add, edit, or import a CSV file.
2. **AI Agent** – the voice and personality of your campaign.\
   The agent defines how to speak, what to do when no one answers, and how to respond in different situations.

Once you link an agent and a contact list, Callin.io takes over: it calls each contact, waits for connection, speaks naturally, listens, and logs the outcome.

## Creating a Campaign

1. Go to [Campaigns](https://app.callin.io/campaigns).
2. Click **Create Campaign**.
3. Enter a descriptive **Campaign Name** (e.g., “October Renewals”).
4. Choose your **AI Agent** — this will handle all conversations.
5. Select **Contacts**:
   - Choose manually, or
   - Upload a CSV file (you can download a sample template).
6. Choose whether to:
   - **Launch Now**, or
   - **Schedule for Later** (set start time, end time, and timezone).
7. Click **Launch** or **Schedule** — and you’re done.

Each campaign will appear in your dashboard with its current status: _Waiting_, _Running_, or _Completed_.

## Understanding Campaign Analytics

Once a campaign starts running, head over to the **Campaigns Analytics** tab.\
You’ll see live performance data, such as:

- Total calls made
- Average call duration
- AMD detection rate (voicemail vs. human)
- Connected call volume
- Conversion or appointment rate

These insights help you improve: maybe your calls are too early, or your agent’s script could be shorter.\
Analytics is your guide for smarter campaigns.

## Helpful Advice

- **Start small** — test before scaling.
- **Name clearly** — “Follow-up Week 42” beats “Test 2”.
- **Respect time zones** — no one likes 3 AM calls.
- **Keep contacts clean** — remove duplicates or outdated numbers.
- **Check analytics often** — small tweaks create big wins.
