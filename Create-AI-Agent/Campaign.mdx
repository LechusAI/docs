## Overview

The **Campaigns** section is where you set up and run outbound calling campaigns, then track how they performed. You’ll find it in the left menu, under **Telephony** ([open it here](https://app.callin.io/campaigns?activeTab=campaigns&view=list)).

From here you can:

- Create a campaign with an **AI Agent**.
- Select contacts, or upload a CSV inside the campaign.
- Launch it now or schedule it for later.
- Turn on follow-up calls for unanswered or busy contacts.
- Monitor progress and results for each contact.
- Review performance in **Campaigns Analytics**.

Click **\+ Create Campaign** to open the **Create Campaign** panel.

### 1) Basic information

Fill in:

- **Campaign Name** (required).
- **AI Agent** (required). You can type to search your agents.

> **Important:** As the panel warns, make sure the agent always has a number assigned during campaign execution. Otherwise, it will not work properly. If it has no number, you’ll see **This agent has no number assigned to it** and the campaign won’t be created. See [Connect Your Number](/Create-AI-Agent/ConnectNumber#assign-the-number-to-the-agent).

### 2) Select contacts

In **Select Contacts**, you can:

- Search with **Search contacts...**.
- Narrow the list with **Filters** (the same advanced filters as in [Contacts](/Create-AI-Agent/Contact)).
- Select contacts with the checkboxes. The header checkbox selects all of them.
- Use **Upload CSV** to bring contacts into the campaign from a file.

At the bottom of the panel you’ll see how many are selected (for example, **2 Contacts Selected**).

> **Tip:** If your contacts are already in a spreadsheet, export them as a **.csv** file before uploading.

### 3) Map columns (when uploading CSV)

If you upload a CSV, you’ll map its columns before continuing, as in [Contacts](/Create-AI-Agent/Contact):

- **Phone** (required)
- **Name**
- **Email**

> **Common mistake:** Not mapping **Phone**. The campaign can’t add contacts without a phone number.

### 4) Launch options

Under **Launch Options**, choose when the campaign runs:

- **Launch Now**: starts immediately.
- **Schedule for Later**: lets you set:
  - **Start Time**: the scheduled campaign will start at this time.
  - **End Time**: if the current time has passed the end time, the campaign is paused until the next day.
  - **Timezone**.

Then click **Launch** (or **Schedule** if you chose **Schedule for Later**). You’ll see **Campaign scheduled successfully**, and the campaign appears in the list.

#### Follow-up Calls

Turn on **Follow-up Calls** to automatically retry calls to contacts who didn’t answer or were busy. Then set:

- **Number of Follow-up Attempts**: from 1 to 10.
- **Retry Intervals (in minutes)**: one field per attempt (**Follow-up #1**, **Follow-up #2**…), counted from the previous attempt. By default: 120, 1440, and 4320 minutes.

The **Summary** shows the result in plain words (for example, after 2 hours, then after 1 day, then after 3 days).

Each campaign card shows its name, its status (for example, **Waiting**), the agent, the number of contacts, and whether it’s **Scheduled**. It has a **View** button and, while it hasn’t started, a **Launch Now** button to start it right away.

### Filter and find campaigns

Click **Filters** to show the filter bar:

- **Campaign ID or Name**.
- **Status**: **Waiting**, **Running**, **Completed**, **Failed**, **Paused**, **Delayed**, or **Stopped**.
- **Date Range**: **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7 Days**, **Last 30 Days**, **This Week**, **This Month**, **Last Month**, or **Custom Range**.
- **Show Deleted**: also shows deleted campaigns.

### View campaign details

Click **View** on a campaign card to open **Campaign Details**:

- The campaign name, its agent, and a **Refresh** button to update results.
- **Campaign Progress**, with a percentage and counters: **Total**, **In Progress**, **Busy**, **No Answer**, **Opted Out**, **Completed**, and **Failed**.
- A table with each contact’s **Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Status** (for example, **Not Started**), and **Outcome**.
- Whether the campaign is **Scheduled** and uses **Follow-up Calls**, and the **Schedule Details** (start time, end time, and timezone).

### Edit a campaign

Click the **⋮** menu on the card and choose **Edit**. The **Edit Campaign** panel has the same fields as when you created it: name, agent, contacts, launch options, and follow-up calls.

### Delete a campaign (permanent)

1. Click the **⋮** menu on the card and choose **Delete**.
2. In **Delete Campaign**, type **DELETE** to confirm and click **Delete**.

If the campaign is scheduled, it won’t make the scheduled calls.

> **Important:** Deleting a campaign is a **permanent action** and cannot be undone.

## Campaigns Analytics

Switch to the **Campaigns Analytics** tab to see how your campaigns performed. Use the selector at the top right to view **All** campaigns or a single one, and the refresh button to update the data.

### What you can review

**Analytics Overview** includes:

- Summary cards: **Total Calls**, **Average Call Duration**, **Total Usage**, **Total Events**, **AMD Detection Rate** (how often an answering machine was detected), and **Success Rate**.
- **Connected Calls Volume**: call activity over time, with the total, the average per day, and the peak day.
- **Appointments by Hour**: scheduled appointments and the peak appointment hour.
- **Call Outcomes** and **Key Metrics** for the selected period.
