## What you can do in Call History

Call History is where you review past calls in your workspace. From here you can:

- **Search and filter** calls by things like phone number, call ID, agent, status, direction, date range, and more.
- **Export** your call list to a **CSV** (and choose which columns to include).
- **Open call details** to review the recording and conversation insights.
- **Download the call audio** (if recording is available for that call).

You can open Call History directly from this link: [Open Call History](https://app.callin.io/call-history)

> **Tip:** Bookmark the link above if you check Call History frequently.

## Open Call History from the sidebar

1. In the left sidebar, go to **Evaluate**.
2. Click **Call History**.

## Filter and search your calls

At the top-right of Call History, you’ll typically see:

- **Filters** (to narrow down what you see)
- **Export CSV** (to download your results)

Inside the filters area, you can use fields like:

- **Phone Number** (type a number)
- **Call ID** (type the call identifier)
- **Handling Agent** (choose an agent or “All”)
- **Status** (for example, completed vs. other statuses)
- **Direction** (inbound vs. outbound, depending on your setup)
- **Appointment**
- **Call Transfer**
- **Machine**
- **Date Range**
- **Call Duration**
- **Call Origin**
- **Campaign ID** (type an ID)

### A practical filtering example

If you want to find calls handled by a specific agent last week:

1. Set **Handling Agent** to the agent’s name.
2. Set **Date Range** to the week you want.
3. (Optional) Add **Status** = “Completed” to focus on finished calls.

> **Common mistake:** Filtering too narrowly (for example, adding both **Call ID** and **Campaign ID**) can result in an empty table. Remove one filter and try again.

## Export Call History to CSV

You can export the calls you’re currently viewing (including any filters you applied).

1. Click **Export CSV**.
2. In the export window, choose the **columns** you want to include.
   - Use **Select All** or **Deselect All** if needed.
3. Click **Export CSV** to download the file.

> **Important:** The CSV export follows your current filters. If you need “all calls,” clear filters first.

## Open call details

To see everything about a specific call:

1. Click on a call row in the table.
2. A **Call Details** panel opens on the right.

In Call Details, you can typically find:

- **Call status** (for example, “Completed”)
- A **Call Recording** player (when available)
- Tabs like **Call Transcript**, **Summary**, **Key Moments**, and **Sentiments**
- A transcript **search** bar and a **Copy** button (availability can vary by account/plan)

> **Tip:** If you’re looking for a specific phrase, use the transcript search to jump right to that part of the conversation.

## Download the call audio (recording)

If the call has a recording, you can download it from the Call Details panel:

1. Click a call row to open **Call Details**.
2. In the **Call Recording** section, click the **three dots (… )** menu.
3. Select **Download Recording**.

You may also see playback **Speed** options (like 0.5x, 0.75x, 1x, 1.25x, 1.5x, 2x) in the same menu.

> **Important:** If you don’t see the download option, recording may be disabled for that call, or access can vary depending on your plan and permissions.
