## How to open Subscription Plans

1. In the left sidebar, go to **Plans & Billing**.
2. Click **Subscription Plans**.

You’ll see plan cards or a plan list depending on your workspace configuration.

## How to open “Manage Subscription” (upgrade, downgrade, cancel)

To manage your current subscription:

1. Click your **email/profile** at the bottom-right of the app.
2. Select **Manage Subscription**.
3. From there, you can typically:
   - **Upgrade** or **downgrade** your plan
   - **Cancel** your subscription

> **Tip:** If you don’t see a specific option (like cancel), it can vary depending on your account, plan, or billing state.

## Key concepts (the parts that matter most)

### Included monthly minutes

Some plans include a monthly bundle of minutes. These minutes are used by your workspace during the billing period.

### Rollover (carry unused minutes)

Unused included minutes can roll over, up to a maximum of **2 months**.

Example (made up):
- Month 1: Your plan includes 1,000 minutes and you use 700 → **300 minutes** unused
- Month 2: You get 1,000 new minutes → you can use **Month 2 minutes + 300 rolled over**
- The rollover balance can grow, but only up to the equivalent of **2 months max**

> **Important:** Rollover is capped at **2 months**. It does not stack indefinitely.

### Overage (what happens when you run out of minutes)

When your **included minutes + rolled over minutes** reach **0**, Callin automatically applies **overage** so your calls can continue.

- **Overage** is billed at the **per-minute rate** shown on your plan.
- Each plan displays its own overage price (for example, $0.12/min, $0.10/min, etc.).

Example (made up):
- Your plan includes 992 minutes and has $0.12/min overage.
- You use 1,050 minutes this month.
- The extra **58 minutes** are billed at **$0.12 per minute**.

> **Common mistake:** Assuming calls will stop at 0 minutes. By default, overage keeps calls running and charges per minute.

### Concurrent calls (call concurrency)

**Concurrent calls** means how many calls can be **active at the same time**.

- If your plan includes **7 concurrent calls**, you can have up to **7 live calls simultaneously**.
- If an 8th call starts while 7 calls are already active, it may be blocked (behavior can vary by configuration).

A helpful way to remember it:
- **Minutes** = total talk time you can use over the month
- **Concurrency** = how many “live call slots” you have right now

### Client accounts (white-label)

**Client accounts** are the number of separate client workspaces you can manage under your white-label setup (common for agencies).

- **Unlimited Plan** and **Growth+ Plan** have **no client account limit**.

> **Tip:** “No limit” can still be subject to fair-use policies depending on account settings.

## Annual billing discount (10%)

If you purchase a plan **annually**, you get a **10% discount** compared to paying month-to-month.

In the UI, you’ll often see:
- “Billed annually”
- A reduced effective monthly price (sometimes shown next to a crossed-out price)

## Plan details

### Pay as you go (Pay-As-You-Go)

This is a pay-per-minute plan where you are billed for usage instead of receiving included minutes.

- **Rate:** $0.16 per minute
- **Included minutes:** none
- **Limit:** **1 agent only**

This option is best if you want a simple setup and pay only for what you use.

### Starter Plan (White Label)

A starter white-label plan designed for smaller agencies.

- Includes monthly minutes (with up to 2 months rollover)
- Has an overage price per minute when minutes run out
- Supports a starter concurrency level
- Includes a limited number of client accounts

### Premium Plan (White Label)

For growing agencies that need more minutes and more simultaneous calls.

- More included minutes than Starter
- Lower overage price than Starter
- Higher concurrency
- More client accounts than Starter

### Unlimited Plan (White Label)

For higher-volume operations needing more concurrency and client flexibility.

- Higher included minutes
- Lower overage price
- Higher concurrency
- **No limit on client accounts**

### Growth+ Plan (White Label)

For enterprise-scale usage with high concurrency and maximum client flexibility.

- Highest included minutes
- Lowest overage price
- Highest concurrency
- **No limit on client accounts**

## Choosing the right plan

- If you frequently hit your minute cap, consider a plan with **more included minutes** or a **lower overage rate**.
- If calls get blocked during peak hours, you likely need higher **concurrent calls**.
- If you manage many customers as an agency, prioritize **client accounts** (Unlimited and Growth+ remove the limit).
