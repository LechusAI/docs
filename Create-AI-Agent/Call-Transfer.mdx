## Overview

Call transfers let your AI agent pass a live call to the right person, team, or AI agent, without hanging up. For example, the agent can send a caller who wants a quote to your sales line, or hand a technical question to a specialized agent.

You set it up in two places:

- **Call Transfer** (left menu, under **Telephony**): your list of **Call Transfer Rules**, each with a name, a number, and a condition.
- The agent’s **Tools** tab: the **Transfer to number** and **Transfer to agent** system tools, which turn transfers on for that agent.

---

## Step 1: Create a Transfer Configuration

In the app, transfer configurations are called **Call Transfer Rules**.

<Frame caption="Video: Call Transfer → Create Rule → fill in Title, Number, and Condition.">
  <video autoPlay muted loop playsInline src="/images/call-transfer-rule.mp4" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step>
    In the left menu, open **Call Transfer** ([open it here](https://app.callin.io/call-transfer)). If you don’t have any rules yet, you’ll see **No Call Transfer Rules found**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click **\+ Create Rule**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Fill in the three fields. All of them are required:
    - **Title**: a name for this rule (for example, _Sales team_ or _Support_).
    - **Number**: the number where the call will be transferred, in E.164 format, with **\+** and the country code (for example, `+15550100200`).
    - **Condition**: when the agent should transfer the call. This lets the LLM know when to transfer the call to this number (for example, _“Transfer the call when the caller asks to speak with sales, wants a price quote or is ready to buy.”_).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Screenshot: a rule that sends sales calls to the sales team.">
  <img src="/images/call-transfer-rule.png" alt="Create Rule window with the Title, Number and Condition fields filled in with an example sales rule, and the Clear, Cancel and Save buttons" />
</Frame>

If a field is empty or wrong, the window tells you: **Title is required**, **Number must be in E.164 format (e.g., \+1234567890)**, or **Transfer condition is required!**. Use **Clear** to empty the form, or **Cancel** to close it without saving.

Your rules appear in the **Call Transfer Rules** list, where you can find them with **Search call transfer rules...**.

## Step 2: Activate Call Transfer for an AI Agent

Transfers are turned on in each agent, in the **System Tools** of the **Tools** tab.

<Frame caption="Video: Tools tab → Transfer to number → Add Rule → Destination Type → Phone Number and Condition.">
  <video autoPlay muted loop playsInline src="/images/call-transfer-agent.mp4" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Go to **AI Agents** and open the agent.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Open the **Tools** tab. On the right, under **System Tools**, turn on **Transfer to number** (“Provides a seamless connection to a live team member when a human touch is needed or requested by the caller”).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    The **Edit Transfer to Number Tool** panel opens. Under **Human Transfer Rules**, click **Add Rule** and fill in:
    - **Destination Type**: **Phone Number** (always the same number) or **Phone Number as Dynamic Variable** (a number that comes from a variable, for example one per contact or per office).
    - **Phone Number** (or **Phone Number as Dynamic Variable**): where the call goes.
    - **Condition**: when to transfer to this number.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Add one rule for each destination. Use **Remove Rule** to delete one.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click **Save** in the panel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Screenshot: a Human Transfer Rule in the Transfer to number tool.">
  <img src="/images/call-transfer-number.png" alt="Edit Transfer to Number Tool panel with Description (optional), Disable interruption, and a Human Transfer Rule with Destination Type Phone Number, an example phone number and a condition" />
</Frame>

The panel also has two optional settings:

- **Description (optional)**: tells the LLM how and when to use the tool. Leave it blank to use the default optimized prompt.
- **Disable interruption**: the caller can’t interrupt while the transfer is running.

<Tip>
  Use the same number and condition you wrote in your **Call Transfer Rules**, so your list in **Call Transfer** and your agents stay consistent.
</Tip>

### Transfer to another AI agent

To pass the call to a more specialized AI agent instead of a person, turn on **Transfer to agent** in the same **System Tools** list. In **Transfer Rules**, click **Add Rule** and choose:

- **Agent**: the agent that takes over the call.
- **Condition**: when to transfer to that agent.
- **Delay before transfer (milliseconds)**: how long to wait before transferring.
- **Transfer message**: the exact phrase the agent says before transferring.
- **Enable First Message**: whether the new agent plays its greeting after the transfer.

## Step 3: Save and Test the Setup

When you change the agent, a bar with **You have unsaved changes** appears at the bottom of the page. Click **Save** to store the changes, or **Clear** to discard them.

### Testing Your Configuration

<Steps>
  <Step>
    In the agent, click **Test AI agent** to talk to it in your browser, or **Test Call** to receive a real call.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Say something that matches one of your conditions (for example, _“I’d like a price quote”_).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Check that the agent transfers the call to the right number or agent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After the call, open **Call History** and use the **Call Transfer** filter to find transferred calls. See [Call History](/Create-AI-Agent/Call-Actions).

## Tips & Best Practices

- **Create one rule per destination.**\
  For example: _Sales_, _Support_, _Billing_. Each one with its own number and condition.
- **Write clear, specific conditions.**\
  The LLM decides when to transfer based on the condition. _“When the caller asks for a refund or a billing change”_ works better than _“billing”_.
- **Use clear, consistent names.**\
  This helps you find rules quickly when you manage many agents.
- **Always use the international format.**\
  Numbers must start with **\+** and the country code (E.164).

## Troubleshooting

**Issue:** I can’t save the rule\
**Cause:** A required field is empty or the number isn’t in E.164 format.\
**Fix:** Fill in **Title**, **Number**, and **Condition**, and write the number with **\+** and the country code.

**Issue:** The agent never transfers\
**Cause:** **Transfer to number** isn’t on, the agent wasn’t saved, or the condition doesn’t match what callers say.\
**Fix:** Check the tool in the **Tools** tab, click **Save** in the agent, and make the condition clearer.

**Issue:** Calls go to the wrong number\
**Cause:** Two rules with overlapping conditions, or a wrong number.\
**Fix:** Make each condition specific and check the numbers in **Human Transfer Rules**.

## Summary

With **Call Transfer Rules** and the **Transfer to number** and **Transfer to agent** tools, your AI agents can route callers to the right person, team, or agent without interruption. This keeps call handling consistent and gives customers a seamless experience.
