## Before You Begin

Make (formerly Integromat) is a visual automation platform that connects Callin.io with thousands of applications without writing code.\
In Make, automations are called **scenarios**. Each scenario links modules, and each module is one step of your process.

The Callin.io app on Make gives you:

- **A trigger, Watch Transcription** (“Get the results of your tasks”): starts a scenario every time Callin.io sends the results of a call.
- **An action, Activate an Agent** (“Activates an agent for calling purpose”): puts one of your Callin.io agents to work from another app, for example to call a new lead.

With them you can:

- Automatically receive call transcriptions.
- Trigger outbound calls from your CRM or web forms.
- Save call data in spreadsheets or databases.
- Notify your team when a call ends.

## Step 1. Access the Integrations Panel in Callin.io

1. Log in to your [Callin.io account](https://app.callin.io).
2. From the left menu, open **Integrations → Actions** ([open it here](https://app.callin.io/actions)).
3. Use the **Automation** filter (or scroll through **All**), find **Make.com** and click **Connect**.

A new tab opens on Make to complete the setup.

## Step 2. Create or Log In to Your Make Account

If you already have a Make account, log in as usual.\
If not, you can create one for free at [make.com](https://www.make.com).

Once inside, you’ll see the **Callin.io** app page, shared by the Callin.io team. It lists the trigger and the action the app offers. Click **Install** to add the app to your Make organization.

## Step 3. Get Your API Key from Callin.io

Make needs your API Key to authenticate your Callin.io account.

1. Open your [Callin.io profile](https://app.callin.io/profile?subSection=api) and go to the **API Keys** tab.
2. If you don’t have a key yet (“No API key found”), click **Regenerate Key** to create one.
3. Use the copy icon to copy the key.

Keep your API Key safe. If it becomes compromised, regenerate it immediately. Remember that regenerating it stops the old key from working, so you’ll need to update the connection in Make.

## Step 4. Connect Callin.io to Make

1. In your Make scenario, click the **+** icon and search for **Callin.io**.
2. Choose a module: **Watch Transcription** or **Activate an Agent**.
3. When Make asks for a connection, click **Add**, paste your API Key and click **Save**.

Your Callin.io account will now appear as connected, and you can reuse that connection in any Callin.io module.

## Step 5. Build Your First Scenario

Let’s create a simple example: saving every call transcription in Google Sheets.

1. In Make, click **Create a new scenario**.
2. Add **Callin.io – Watch Transcription** as the first module (the trigger).
3. Add a second module: **Google Sheets – Add a Row**.
4. Select your sheet and map the Callin.io fields to your spreadsheet columns.
5. Save the scenario and turn it on.

From now on, every completed call will automatically send its transcription to your sheet.

## Advanced Configuration

With Make, you can combine multiple modules and conditions to create powerful automations:

- **Start your agents from other apps**: add **Callin.io – Activate an Agent** after a trigger from your CRM or form tool, so an agent calls each new lead.
- Send Slack notifications when specific criteria are met.
- Store transcriptions in Google Drive or another cloud service.

You start the connection from [Integrations → Actions → Make.com](https://app.callin.io/actions) in your Callin.io dashboard, and you manage your scenarios in Make.

## Common Issues and Solutions

**Connection fails when entering the API Key.**\
Make sure there are no extra spaces when pasting. If the issue persists, regenerate the key from the [API Keys section](https://app.callin.io/profile?subSection=api) and update the connection in Make.

**I can’t find Callin.io when searching in Make.**\
Install the app first: click **Connect** on **Make.com** in [Integrations → Actions](https://app.callin.io/actions) and then **Install** on the Callin.io app page.

**Scenario doesn’t receive data.**\
Check that the first module is **Callin.io – Watch Transcription** and that the scenario is turned on.

**Scenario doesn’t run automatically.**\
Ensure it’s saved and turned on. You can test it manually with **Run once**.

## Final Note

Integrating Callin.io with Make allows you to design custom workflows visually and automate communication tasks effortlessly.\
Once your first scenario is up and running, you’ll find it easy to expand and connect Callin.io to any other system you use daily.
