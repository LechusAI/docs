## Overview

Welcome to Callin.io! When you sign up, you'll automatically get a Free Trial with enough minutes to explore all the core features. If you’re ready for more, upgrading your plan takes just a couple of clicks.

## Steps

1. **Open Your Profile Menu**\
   Look to the bottom-left of your dashboard and click your profile icon. A menu will pop up showing your current usage status.
   - **New here?** You’ll see a **Free Trial** button.
   - **Already tried it?** You’ll see **Upgrade** instead.

   Click that button to head over to the Plans page.
2. **Check Out the Plans**\
   You’ll see a range of options — pick the one that matches your needs:
   - 🔹 **Basic Plan** **(\$30/mo)**— 215 minutes/month, supports multiple languages. Great for solo users.
   - 🔹 **Starter Plan (\$119/mo)** — Up to 5 clients, white-labeled with your branding.
   - 🔹 **Premium Plan (\$299/mo)** — More minutes and extra customization options.
   - 🔹 **Unlimited Plan (\$499/mo)** — No limits on usage or clients.
   - 🔹 **Growth Plan (\$1499/mo)** — Built for scale, full white-label control.
3. **Click and Subscribe**\
   Hit **Subscribe** on the plan you want. Just follow the payment steps — once confirmed, you’ll instantly see your updated balance and plan details in the dashboard.

## Advanced Setup (Optional)

If you’re using a white-label plan, you can also:

- Upload your own logo and favicon.
- Use a custom domain.
- Connect Stripe for billing your clients.
- Set minute limits per client account.

Need help? Just search for the White Label Setup Guide.

## Troubleshooting

**No Free Trial showing?**\
You’ve probably already used it — you’ll only see the Upgrade option now.

**Payment not working?**\
Double-check your card or contact support.

**Plan not updating?**\
Try refreshing the page or logging out and back in. It usually syncs right away.
