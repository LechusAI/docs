## What Is an AI Call Agent

An AI Call Agent is an intelligent voice assistant that can talk, listen, and act like a trained human representative. It answers or makes calls automatically. It greets callers, understands what they need, and takes action.

Agents handle real conversations 24/7. They reduce missed calls and free your team from repetitive phone work. They sound natural, follow your instructions, and can speak multiple languages.

## Connecting a Phone Number

Every agent needs a phone number to make or receive calls. Go to **Numbers** and click **+ Add Number**. You have several options:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Purchase Number" icon="cart-shopping">
    Buy a new number directly in your Callin.io workspace.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Import from Twilio" icon="arrow-right-to-bracket">
    Bring the numbers you already own in Twilio.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Add Telnyx Number" icon="sim-card">
    Import your Telnyx numbers once Telnyx is connected.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Add Personal Number" icon="mobile-screen">
    Verify a number you already use, such as your business line.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

You can also bring your own carrier with **BYOC (SIP Trunk)**.

Once your number is active, your agent uses it like a real phone line, with caller ID, voicemail detection, and call history.

> **Tip:** Learn every option step by step in [Numbers And BYOC](/Create-AI-Agent/Configure-Numbers).

## Understanding Call Usage and Costs

Each call uses two types of minutes:

1. **Carrier minutes**: charged by the phone provider (Twilio, Telnyx, or your own carrier) for the phone connection.
2. **Callin AI minutes**: cover the AI that listens, thinks, and responds during the call.

<Note>
**Example:** a 5-minute call = 5 carrier minutes + 5 Callin AI minutes.
</Note>

When you buy a number through Callin.io, there is **no extra platform fee** for the number itself.

> **Important:** Calls made with numbers bought through Callin.io and our carrier have a **1-minute minimum charge**. A 20-second call is billed as 1 minute. After the first minute, you pay the actual duration. This rule does not apply to browser test calls, imported numbers (Twilio or Telnyx), or your own SIP trunk.

You can check your total minutes on **Home** (**Total Usage**). To see your plan or add credits, open **Upgrade Plan**.

## Integrations and Webhooks

Your AI Call Agent doesn't have to work alone. It connects to the tools you already use for scheduling, CRM, and automation. You'll find them all in **Actions** in the sidebar.

Many integrations use **webhooks**: simple URLs that receive data from each call in real time. A webhook sends structured information to any system that can receive HTTP requests, such as caller details, extracted data, timestamps, or transcripts.
