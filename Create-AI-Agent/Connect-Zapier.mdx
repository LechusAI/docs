## Before You Begin

Zapier is a platform that connects different apps so they can work together automatically. These workflows are called Zaps.\
Each Zap follows a simple rule: when something happens in one app, an action runs in another.

For instance, when your Callin.io agent finishes a call, Zapier can save the transcription in Google Sheets, send it by email, or post it in Slack.

The Callin.io app on Zapier gives you:

- **A trigger, Get Transcription**: starts a Zap every time Callin.io sends the transcription of a call.
- **An action, Activate Agent**: makes one of your Callin.io agents start working from another app (for example, to call a new lead from your CRM or form tool).

With them you can:

- Automatically receive call transcriptions.
- Trigger outbound calls from your CRM or form tools.
- Connect Callin.io with thousands of compatible applications.

## Step 1. Open the Integrations Panel in Callin.io

1. Log in to your [Callin.io account](https://app.callin.io).
2. From the left menu, open **Integrations → Actions** ([open it here](https://app.callin.io/actions)).
3. Use the **Automation** filter (or scroll through **All**), find **Zapier** and click **Connect**.

A new tab opens on Zapier to complete the setup.

## Step 2. Sign In or Create Your Zapier Account

If you already have a Zapier account, log in as usual.\
If not, you can create one for free at [zapier.com](https://zapier.com).

Once inside, you’ll see the page **“You have been invited to use Callin.io on Zapier!”**, sent by the Callin.io team. Click **Accept Invite & Build a Zap** to enable the integration. Zapier opens the Zap editor.

## Step 3. Get Your API Key from Callin.io

Zapier needs to verify your account securely using an API Key.

1. Open your [Callin.io profile](https://app.callin.io/profile) and go to the **API Keys** tab.
2. If you don’t have a key yet (“No API key found”), click **Regenerate Key** to create one.
3. Use the copy icon to copy the key.

Keep your API Key safe. If it becomes compromised, regenerate it immediately. Remember that regenerating it stops the old key from working, so you’ll need to reconnect your account in Zapier.

## Step 4. Authorize the Connection in Zapier

1. In a Callin.io step of your Zap, go to **Account** and click **Sign in** (or **Change** → **Connect a new account**).
2. A Zapier window asks for access to your Callin.io account. Paste your API Key and confirm.

Your Callin.io account will now appear as connected in **Account**.

If you see **“This account connection is expired,”** click **Reconnect this account** and enter your current key again.

## Step 5. Build Your First Zap

Let’s create your first automation: receiving call transcriptions automatically.

1. In Zapier, click **Create** → **Zaps**.
2. Click **Trigger**, search for **callin** and choose **Callin.io (1.0.1)**.
3. In **Trigger event**, choose **Get Transcription** (“Triggers when receives transcription data from external service”).
4. Select your Callin.io account and click **Continue**.

Now choose what happens next. For example:

- Save the transcription to Google Sheets.
- Send a message in Slack.
- Create a record in your CRM such as HubSpot or Zoho.

Every time a call ends, Zapier will automatically perform the chosen action.

> **Tip:** When you search for **callin**, you may see two versions: **Callin.io (1.0.1)** and **Callin.io (1.0.2)**, which appears as **Callin** with a **Legacy** label. Use **Callin.io (1.0.1)**: it has the **Get Transcription** trigger and the **Activate Agent** action.

## Advanced Configuration

You can do much more with this integration:

- **Start your agents from other apps**: add a Callin.io step as an action and choose **Activate Agent** (“Activates an Agent on Callin.io”). For example, activate an agent every time a new lead is created in your CRM or form tool.
- Sync call data with Airtable, Notion, or analytics platforms.
- Send events to marketing tools for reporting or tracking.

## Common Issues and Solutions

**Zap not triggering.**\
Check that your trigger event is correctly set, that the Callin.io account is connected and that the Zap is published.

**Invalid API Key or authentication error.**\
Regenerate a new key from the [API Keys section](https://app.callin.io/profile) and reconnect your account.

**“This account connection is expired.”**\
Click **Reconnect this account** and paste your current API Key.

**App appears as “Draft.”**\
This simply means the integration is shared by invitation and is still in beta. It remains fully functional.

**I don’t see the Get Transcription trigger.**\
Make sure you chose **Callin.io (1.0.1)** and not the **Legacy** version.

## Final Note

By connecting Callin.io and Zapier, you can eliminate repetitive tasks and centralize your call data effortlessly.\
Once you build your first Zap, you’ll see how easy it is to automate call workflows, share transcriptions, and streamline your operations across all your favorite tools.
