## Overview

The **Contacts** section is where your AI Agents’ conversations begin.  
Every campaign in Callin.io relies on a list of people — potential leads, customers, or participants — stored and managed here.

You can think of Contacts as your **AI-ready phonebook**: it’s the foundation of every [Campaign](https://app.callin.io/campaigns) you launch.  
Without contacts, your agents don’t have anyone to talk to — so keeping this list organized and accurate is key.

---

## How Contacts Work

Each contact contains the essentials your AI Agent needs to start a conversation:
- **Name:** used to personalize greetings and responses.  
- **Phone number:** required for calls.  
- **Email:** optional, but useful for reference or integrations.

You can filter contacts by **agent**, letting you prepare separate lists for different use cases — sales, follow-ups, reminders, or surveys.

---

## Why Contacts Matter

Contacts are more than just phone numbers — they’re the people your AI connects with.  
Keeping this space clean, updated, and well-structured means:
- More successful calls.  
- Less wasted time on invalid numbers.  
- Better reporting when you analyze your [Campaigns](https://app.callin.io/campaigns).  

For example:
- A **sales team** might upload leads collected from a form.  
- A **support team** might add customers waiting for a callback.  
- A **marketing team** could build lists for product announcements.

Everything your agent says or tracks flows from this page.

---

## Adding and Managing Contacts

There are two main ways to create or update your contact list:

1. **Add Contact Manually**  
   - Click **Add Contact**.  
   - Fill in *Name* and *Phone Number* (required), plus *Email* if available.  
   - Click **Save** — your new contact appears instantly.

2. **Upload a CSV File**  
   - Click **Upload CSV**.  
   - You can drag and drop your file or click **Select File**.  
   - Use the downloadable **template** to ensure column names (name, phoneNumber, email) match the format.  
   - Max file size: 10 MB.

Once uploaded, all your contacts will display in the table below, ready to be linked to your [Campaigns](https://app.callin.io/campaigns).

---

## Editing, Calling, or Deleting Contacts

Each contact row offers quick actions:
- 👁 **View** – open the contact details.  
- ✏️ **Edit** – change information inline.  
- 🗑 **Delete Selected** – remove contacts in bulk.  
- 📞 **Call Selected** – trigger an instant call through the selected AI Agent.  

You can also **export your list** as CSV anytime using **Export to CSV**, perfect for backups or sharing with other workspaces.

---

## Best Practices

- Keep phone numbers formatted with country code (e.g., `+1`, `+34`).  
- Group contacts by purpose — campaigns work best with targeted lists.  
- Periodically clean duplicates or outdated entries.  
- Always double-check before launching calls to ensure accuracy.
